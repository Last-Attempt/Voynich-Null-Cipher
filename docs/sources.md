# Sources and scope of the literature check

René Zandbergen's [transliteration overview](https://www.voynich.nu/transcr.html), [STA explanation and downloads](https://www.voynich.nu/extra/sta.html) and [IVTFF 2.0 specification](https://www.voynich.nu/software/ivtt/IVTFF_format.pdf) define the files and parsing conventions used here. Original files, headers, retrieval date, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are preserved in [the manifest](../data/manifest.json). ZL3b identifies version 3b dated 13 May 2025; RF1b is the combined reference rendering, and IT2a is the converted older Takahashi rendering. These are transcription hypotheses, not recovered plaintext.

The initial targeted search on 14 October 2026 found related null-removal experiments in [Koen Gheuens' Entropy Hunting IV: nulls](https://herculeaf.wordpress.com/2020/09/28/entropy-hunting-iv-nulls/) and broader script-unit/entropy work in [Lindemann and Bowern, Character Entropy in Modern and Historical Texts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14697) and [Reddy and Knight, What We Know About the Voynich Manuscript](https://aclanthology.org/W11-1511/). This was a bounded discovery search, not a systematic literature review. It did not establish that the exact six-mask, two-offset, matched-deletion design is unprecedented; no priority claim is made. The null-removal post is an independent research blog, not peer-reviewed confirmation; its author added a warning that some numerical values were faulty, so none are reused here.

Search also surfaced a recent preprint, [Rozanova and Temerev, A Glyph Is Not a Letter, a Token Is Not a Word, a Space Is Not a Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17096). It is a lead for subsequent review, not a replicated finding of VOY. No published study's results are substituted for our calculations.
